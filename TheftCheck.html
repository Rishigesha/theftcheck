About TheftCheck
Learn how our platform protects consumers worldwide

OUR MISSION

TheftCheck is a next-generation phone verification platform designed to protect consumers from fraudulent phone purchases. We connect buyers and sellers to a global network of manufacturer registries, telecom blacklists, and law enforcement databases to ensure every device transaction is transparent and trustworthy.

Our platform helps you verify whether a phone is brand new, previously used, or has been reported as stolen — all in seconds, from anywhere in the world.

HOW IT WORKS

When you submit a device's IMEI or serial number, TheftCheck performs a multi-layer verification process. First, it validates the format and checks the manufacturer's registry to confirm the device's authenticity. Then it cross-references against a stolen device database containing blacklisted IMEIs from telecom companies and police records.

The system also checks activation history — whether the phone has been previously activated, sold, or refurbished — helping determine its true condition and status before you finalize any purchase or sale.

DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY

All checks are handled through secure, encrypted API integrations with manufacturer and government databases. We do not permanently store personal data or device information unless you explicitly consent. Our systems use end-to-end encryption, and stolen device lists are continuously synchronized through real-time feeds from partner agencies.

DATABASE PARTNERS

TheftCheck integrates with manufacturer registries (Apple, Samsung, Google, Xiaomi, OPPO, and 200+ more), national telecom blacklists, INTERPOL's Stolen and Lost Travel Documents database extension for devices, and cybercrime units across 180 countries. Our partnerships are continuously expanding to provide the most comprehensive coverage available.
